# Project Overview

Project Name: Petopia Designer

Description: Petopia Designer is a web application that allows users to design and customize virtual habitats for their digital companions. Users can select from a variety of pets (cats, dogs, dragons, and more!), choose from a range of decorations and interactive elements, and create unique, stylish homes. A simulated subscription model unlocks access to exclusive content and features. Users can also share their designs with friends (via a mocked sharing flow).

Features:

*   Pet Selection: Choose from a variety of virtual pets, each with unique design considerations.
*   Drag-and-Drop Habitat Designer: An intuitive drag-and-drop interface for arranging decorations and interactive elements.
*   Diverse Decoration Library: A wide selection of furniture, plants, toys, and other decorative items.
*   Interactive Elements: Add interactive elements like scratching posts, water bowls, and toys that pets can "use."
*   AI-Powered Design Suggestions: (Mocked) Get smart suggestions for decorations and layouts based on your chosen pet and habitat style.
*   Subscription Model: (Mocked) Unlock exclusive decorations, interactive elements, and design themes with a premium subscription.
*   Social Sharing: (Mocked) Share your habitat designs with friends and the Petopia community.
*   Responsive Design: Ensure the app looks great on any device.

Technology Stack:

*   Cloudflare next-on-pages framework (NextJS) for the web application.
